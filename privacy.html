Core Breach Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

Core Breach is developed by Helfrich Photography. This policy explains how the game handles your information.

Data we collect

None. Core Breach does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.

Saved games

Your progress (gear, inventory, settings) is stored only on your device using your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Permissions

The game requests no special device permissions. An internet connection is only used to download and update the game itself.

Children

Because no data is collected, the game collects no information from children.

Changes

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
